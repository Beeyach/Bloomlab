| D-021 | 2026-09-02 | `HoloMaterial` is modelled on the foil trading card in the Doodlemon puzzle view (a photographed holo card with a gentle float), not on the landing-page tiles (which only lift on hover). | D-022 | 2026-09-02 | HoloMaterial smoothing (follow and settle) is exponential easing in a `requestAnimationFrame` loop that writes seven custom properties and stops when the pose converges; the stylesheet has no transitions on the pointer-driven properties. | In the production bundle, CSS transitions on properties whose values come from `var()`-driven `calc()` never advanced (transform, band position, glare and shadow all stayed at their pre-hover values on the deployed preview while `transition: none` fixed them instantly); the dev server did not reproduce it. Easing in JS behaves identically in dev, in production and in tests, gives a spring-like feel, and costs a few multiplications per frame only while a card is moving. |
| D-023 | 2026-09-02 | From a live study of the reference (real pointer): its tiles lift 8 px with a deeper shadow and a pastel gradient ring on hover, its foil card is a photograph with a slow float, and the only pointer-driven motion is a background parallax. Bloomlab adopts the *tactile* half — the hover lift is 5 px and the shadow deepens with it, and the rim light is tinted with the palette so the edge reads as iridescent foil — and keeps its own procedural material and spec physics (tilt, glare, spectral shift, settle). No idle float, no parallax, no copied gradient values. | Spec §62 asks to match the reference's tactile, holographic *feeling* and forbids copying art or composition; §148 / MOT-004 forbid constant animation. The lift-and-shadow cue is what makes the reference's hover feel physical; the tinted rim answers "iridescence" without a photograph. |

| D-030 | 2026-09-02 | Revision semantics: the server assigns `revision` when it accepts a write (1 on insert, +1 per accepted change); a client adopts the server number at each sync point and bumps it locally between syncs; a push carries `base_revision` (the last server revision the client saw, kept in `sync_shadow`). | D-031 | 2026-09-02 | The device keeps its canonical sync key in IndexedDB (`device.sync_key`) so the learner can show, copy or re-download it; requests carry only the per-device session token (`device.session_token`). | D-043 | 2026-09-02 | Learner evidence is append-only; skill progress, campaign progress and the review queue are *derived* rows recomputed from evidence on every device (and after every sync that pulled something), never authored. | D-044 | 2026-09-02 | The mastery ladder is earned from kinds of evidence and assistance, never from a score threshold: LEARNING on any evidence, GUIDED on a pass with any help, PRACTICED on a pass with at most light help, INDEPENDENT on an unassisted pass, PRESSURE_TESTED on an unassisted pressure pass, MASTERED on the skill's declared count of unassisted passes (never fewer than two, over two distinct demonstrations) plus every declared requirement. | D-045 | 2026-09-02 | Assistance rolls up from hint use by a fixed table: worked example → heavy; concept reminder or three nudges → guided; one or two nudges → light; nothing → independent. A guided-practice exercise (or one authored in `guided` mode) is guided at least. Only `independent` passes of independent-capable kinds count toward INDEPENDENT and MASTERED; light-assisted passes count toward PRACTICED. | Spec §28 and §34 (a heavily assisted pass is not independent evidence; mastery requires sufficient independent evidence) made explicit and testable (MAS-011). |
| D-046 | 2026-09-02 | NEEDS_REFRESH is an overlay, not a demotion: the earned ladder state stays in the record as `refresh_from`, and a passed retrieval restores it because evaluation is a pure function of the evidence history. A skill needs refresh when its review is overdue by more than 14 days or its latest attempt is a failed retrieval, and only a retrieval passed with at most light assistance clears it; review intervals are 10 / 21 / 35 / 60 days by earned state, divided by importance and shortened by the recent failure rate, never below 3 days. | Spec §32 and TA§69: old skills resurface, review never blocks, and historical mastery evidence is never erased. |
| D-047 | 2026-09-02 | Prerequisite satisfaction and gate availability read only the earned ladder state (INDEPENDENT or above), never the clock: a refresh-due prerequisite still unlocks what depends on it, and any skill whose prerequisites are satisfied is available wherever it sits in the campaign (`work_ahead`). | D-048 | 2026-09-02 | The session builder is a fixed order of decisions with budget shares: due retrieval first (≤ 20 % of the budget, never blocking), then repair (weak prerequisites of the next work, recent failures, and assisted passes when assistance dependence ≥ 0.5), then the learner's focus, then the current gate's required skills and available work-ahead skills, then pending fieldwork and the active project. | D-049 | 2026-09-02 | Every attempt and evidence record stores the version stamp `{app, content, content_hash, simulator, rules}` at write time and is never rewritten; recompute may change derived rows (which record the content version they were computed under) but never evidence. | D-050 | 2026-09-02 | `last_demonstrated` (the anchor of every review interval) is the latest pass at the PRACTICED bar or better — an independent-capable kind with at most light assistance. A guided or worked-example pass neither restores a NEEDS_REFRESH skill nor postpones its review. Rules bumped to `2026.09.02-r2`. | Found by the explicit refresh round-trip test before merge: with any pass counting, a worked-example retrieval cleared NEEDS_REFRESH and reset the review clock, contradicting MAS-011. |
| D-051 | 2026-09-02 | What resets the review clock is an explicit rule, `DEMONSTRATION_RULES` / `isDemonstration`: a `passed` attempt of a demonstration kind (deterministic or independent exercise, pressure test, explanation, sales use, fieldwork or real-GHL proof — only with the proof provided — and retrieval) with no critical failure and at most light assistance. Rules bumped to `2026.09.02-r3`. | The user asked for the rule to be explicit rather than incidental; making fieldwork proof part of `isPass` keeps the ladder, counts and review clock consistent with spec §30 "real-GHL evidence where required". |
| D-052 | 2026-09-03 | Retrieval is a review demonstration, not a progression kind: `INDEPENDENT_KINDS` (deterministic and independent exercises, pressure tests, explanation, sales use, fieldwork, real-GHL) drive INDEPENDENT and MASTERED; `REVIEW_DEMONSTRATION_KINDS` (those plus retrieval) drive `last_demonstrated` and the NEEDS_REFRESH overlay. Rules bumped to `2026.09.03-r4`. | Spec §32 describes retrieval as short challenges that keep old skills alive; letting a five-minute retrieval count as the second demonstration toward MASTERED over-credited it. |
