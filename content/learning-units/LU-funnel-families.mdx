## Choose the smallest useful path

A funnel family describes the customer's decision, not a template to install everywhere. Start with intent, what the business must learn and the next operational action. The same business may need several paths for different audiences. Compare each path below, then design the actual sequence for the associated brief.

## lead capture

An interested visitor asks for a reply. Capture enough contact information for a named follow-up owner; confirm receipt and state the response expectation. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## lead magnet

An early visitor wants useful information. Deliver the promised resource before asking for a sales conversation; obtain appropriate permission for further follow-up. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## consultation

The service needs diagnosis before recommendation. Explain the purpose, qualification and limits of the conversation; capture the lead before the calendar. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## appointment

The visitor is ready to reserve a known service. Show eligibility, availability, location, cancellation terms and the booking confirmation. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## quote request

The business must estimate variable work. Collect the factors that materially change the estimate and define inspection and response expectations. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## application

The business must select a suitable buyer. Ask decision-relevant questions, explain why, and route eligible and ineligible applicants honestly. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## assessment

The visitor needs a structured starting diagnosis. Explain what the assessment can establish and how uncertain results will be discussed. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## vsl to call

The offer needs explanation before a conversation. Let the video make a bounded promise, provide a text alternative, and link to an appropriate call. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## content to booking

Useful content establishes relevance. Connect the specific article or demonstration to its logical next appointment, without replacing the content with a pitch. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## service inquiry

The visitor must establish whether the provider can help. Clarify service, geography and response expectation; do not force an exact appointment before fit is known. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## reactivation

A known former lead may have a renewed need. Use only an appropriate contactable audience, acknowledge context, offer a relevant next step and stop when they decline. Keep the promise visible at the point where the visitor gives information, and make the next owner or system action explicit.

## Boundaries before sophistication

A family is not a guarantee of conversion. Preserve unknowns, define a measurement window and review operational capacity before buying traffic. Practitioner work adds paid offers and deeper qualification; Advanced adds multi-offer orchestration; Specialist adds complex architecture. Those are future tier boundaries, not prerequisites for these Field Ready decisions.
