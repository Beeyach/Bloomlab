# Trace ecommerce and affiliate obligations

## A purchase begins several obligations

An ecommerce storefront lets customers browse products and purchase them. Product configuration and price are only part of the journey: payment, inventory, fulfillment, customer notifications and returns have separate states. The Payments Lab models a narrow synthetic charge lifecycle, not a complete ecommerce operation.

HighLevel products can be used in online stores and other selling experiences. Select the appropriate channel and verify that the intended product is actually available there. A product existing in Payments does not by itself prove that the storefront, shipping or downloadable file is configured correctly.

## Affiliate attribution is not immediate payout

Affiliate Manager uses campaign sources and tracking links to associate referred activity with an affiliate. An attributed sale, an approved commission and a paid commission are distinct facts. Define attribution policy, qualifying products, refund treatment, review period and payout responsibility before inviting promoters.

In this fictional policy, commissions become eligible only after successful payment and the review window, and a refunded sale does not qualify. This is a training contract, not a claim that every native campaign automatically follows the same rules. Check the configured campaign and transaction evidence.

## Verify the complete commercial chain

Test a failed payment, duplicate checkout, refunded order, missing fulfillment and an affiliate claiming the wrong campaign. Do not report gross attributed revenue as profit or imply that tracking proves a causal contribution. Make media and promotion permissions explicit; an affiliate may not invent claims on the business's behalf.

The practical separates an order's states and computes a commission under a supplied rule. It neither launches a store nor tracks a real visitor or pays an affiliate. Any real fulfillment, return, tax or commercial policy needs the business owner's review; this specialty introduces the system boundaries without manufacturing a full commerce Lab.
