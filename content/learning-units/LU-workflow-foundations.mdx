## An event happens

Nothing runs until something happens: a form is submitted, a tag is added, an appointment is
booked. In GHL that event is the workflow's **trigger**. A trigger can carry **filters** — this
form, this calendar, this tag — and a contact only enrolls when the event matches them.

<Feature id="GHL-WF-FORM-SUBMITTED" />

## Then the actions run, top to bottom

Once enrolled, the contact walks down the list of actions. `Send SMS` sends a message. `Add
Contact Tag` records that something happened. `Wait` pauses the contact — for a set period, until
a date, or relative to an appointment — and `If/Else` sends the contact down the first branch
whose condition matches, or down the None branch when nothing does.

<Depth title="Merge fields: the contact's data inside the message">
  A template like `Hi {{contact.first_name}}, you're booked on {{appointment.start_time}}` is
  filled in per contact at send time. If the field is empty, the message goes out with a gap —
  which is why required form fields matter to automation, not just to the CRM.
</Depth>
