# Operate reviews and Social Planner responsibly

## Reputation is not a manufactured outcome

HighLevel Reputation supports review-request messaging and tracking. The current request guide distinguishes templates, initial timing and retry slots; requesting a review is not evidence that a review was written. Clicking a destination link is another separate event. Report each honestly and respect the customer's communication preferences.

A business should ask for genuine feedback under the relevant destination's policies. Do not fabricate reviews, suppress legitimate criticism through misleading routing or promise a guaranteed star rating. Escalate a complaint to the responsible person without publishing private customer details in a reply. This training policy requires human review of sensitive public responses.

## Social Planner has its own lifecycle

Social Planner connects social accounts and supports drafting, scheduling, publishing and performance tracking. Available formats and analytics vary by connected platform. A saved draft is not a published post, and a scheduled post can fail if a connection expires or an asset is invalid.

Keep a publishing brief with audience, approved claims, media rights, destination, timezone and owner. Test the preview and confirm the actual publication result. If an account disconnects, repair the connection with its owner and check whether the original post already published before resubmitting. Do not create duplicate public content to clear a queue.

## A small maintenance contract

Review the request cadence, sender, destination link and stop conditions. For social work, check connection health, failed posts and the approved content schedule. Keep engagement metrics distinct from sales attribution. A useful report shows what happened and what still needs verification.

The local practical classifies request outcomes and repairs a publishing failure. It sends no review request, connects no social account and publishes nothing. These specialties are represented in the master graph through instruction and contract work, not a fabricated live Reputation or Social Planner Lab.
