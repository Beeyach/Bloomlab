# Publish blogs and interpret SEO evidence

## Publishing and discovery are different

HighLevel Blogs organises posts, authors and categories within a site's publishing experience. A draft, published URL, crawlable page and indexed search result are different states. Decide which one your evidence establishes before reporting success.

Set the intended domain, slug, title, description and useful content. Check links, headings, image alternatives and the customer-facing call to action. An incorrect domain or broken destination cannot be repaired by repeating a keyword. Publishing a post also does not prove that a search engine has crawled or ranked it.

## Know which SEO surface you are using

HighLevel's SEO feature is an integrated service powered by Search Atlas, with account enablement, quotas and billing boundaries. Blog metadata is not the same as purchasing or configuring that broader service. Verify access and current terms before promising an audit or ongoing package.

An audit can reveal missing metadata, broken links or other technical opportunities. Its score does not establish business impact, and a change in organic traffic may have several causes. Separate observed issues, plausible hypotheses and unknowns. Never guarantee a ranking or create invented performance evidence to make a monthly report look complete.

## A reviewable publishing loop

Start with an audience question and an approved claim set. Draft, fact-check, review rights and brand details, preview, publish with approval, then verify the final URL. Maintain a change log and monitor actual results over comparable windows. If a URL changes, plan the destination and check links rather than leaving the old path to fail silently.
