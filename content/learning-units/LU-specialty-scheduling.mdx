# Choose rentals, services and resource capacity

## Choose the object being booked

A service appointment combines a service, time, staff and any required physical resource. A rental reserves a listing or asset across its permitted duration and inventory rules. A class reserves a seat in a shared session. These may all appear near scheduling, but they are not interchangeable capacity models.

HighLevel Rentals supports rental listings and multi-duration booking needs, with inventory and pricing configuration. Services has its own staff and service views. Verify the intended module and available settings before promising a single template will cover a massage, a group class and a weekend equipment rental.

## Capacity has a unit

A room resource with capacity one cannot support two overlapping service bookings, even if two staff members are available. Equivalent alternatives mean one free resource may be selected; they do not mean the booking requires every alternative at once. A rental with three tracked units has an inventory constraint separate from a service host's calendar.

The advanced Calendar Lab simulates classes and a deliberately narrower resource model. It does not implement multi-day Rentals, rental pricing or location inventory. Use that Lab to understand overlap and cancellation, then identify the native configuration still needed for a real rental deployment.

## Rehearse the return and release

Test overlapping requests, cancellation, changed duration, unavailable staff and a resource removed from service. For a rental, include return timing and inventory availability in the acceptance plan. Do not assume a paid deposit or a displayed opening guarantees all required assets exist.

The practical selects a module for a supplied business and computes a resource conflict. It is a local architecture decision, not an actual rental reservation. The graph deliberately links this specialty to the account-backed class/resource prerequisite while preserving the limits of both. No external calendar, rental listing or paid booking is created.
