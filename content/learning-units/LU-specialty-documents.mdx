# Separate agreements, estimates and billing states

## Different documents answer different questions

Documents & Contracts manages agreements and their document lifecycle. Estimates communicate a proposed commercial amount before final billing. An invoice requests payment from a particular customer. A payment link offers a checkout route, and a subscription represents recurring billing obligations. None of those names is interchangeable with “the client paid”.

HighLevel's Estimates documentation supports acceptance/rejection and conversion to an invoice. Documents & Contracts has its own sent, viewed and completion evidence. A viewed document is not necessarily signed; an accepted estimate is not a settled invoice. Verify which event the workflow actually consumes.

## Review before sending

Select the correct customer, version, scope, amount, currency and billing schedule. Check the approval owner and whether the operation creates a draft or sends immediately. Under this training policy, a new commercial document requires owner review before sending; that policy is not a claim that every native feature imposes the same gate.

Contract enforceability and tax treatment are outside this lesson. Have the business obtain qualified review for its actual terms and jurisdiction. Do not infer legal acceptance merely from a synthetic state field or a platform button label.

## Changes have downstream consequences

A payment failure, refund, cancellation or scope amendment can require different follow-up. Refunding one charge does not automatically terminate every recurring agreement. Keep the subscription status and the individual transaction status separate, and record who may make each change.

Rehearse a viewed-but-unsigned agreement, accepted-but-unpaid estimate, failed invoice, refunded recurring charge and an inactive payment link. The Payments Lab supplies a narrow synthetic lifecycle; this specialty adds the document and commercial-boundary reasoning. The practical creates no legally operative document, sends no invoice and moves no money. It checks only the supplied evidence and approval contract.
