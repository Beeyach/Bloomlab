## Separate the observation from its cause

Write the symptom as something another person can reproduce: a lead received two reminders, a visitor never reached booking, or an owner cannot see the next action. Name the affected record, event and time window. A hypothesis such as “re-entry is enabled” is a proposed cause, not the symptom. Inspect the execution log, stored fields, messages and funnel telemetry before changing configuration.

Start from a baseline run, change one relevant thing, repeat the same case and compare the observable output. Also rerun the neighbouring case the fix might break. A missing phone and do-not-disturb can both produce no SMS, but they need different explanations. A provider acceptance does not prove inbox or handset delivery. Review sender setup, permissions and delivery information available in the real account; preserve unknown delivery outcomes instead of reporting success from a workflow action alone.

## Diagnose the funnel as a sequence

Use actual visits, completions, bookings and attended calls for the same cohort. Identify the stage losing people and keep the denominator visible. A small sample can suggest a test; it cannot prove a durable uplift. Form a bounded hypothesis, choose one intervention, define its comparison window and guardrails, then inspect both conversion and operational consequences. Changing traffic quality and page copy simultaneously makes causal interpretation harder.

## Bloomwired pre-launch protocol

For every area below, record the setup, expected behaviour, observed behaviour and evidence reference. A blocked test remains blocked. A failed required test must be corrected and rerun before launch. Where the simulator does not implement a native feature, name that boundary and perform the corresponding manual training-account inspection; do not claim the simulation tested it.

- **pages:** Open every published training page and inspect the intended offer and destination.
- **forms:** Submit a new fictional record and repeat the enquiry with a changed answer.
- **links:** Follow each CTA, confirmation, cancellation and reschedule link to its intended destination.
- **validation:** Submit empty and invalid required values, then correct them and retry.
- **field mapping:** Compare each submitted answer with its stored field and verify the repeated value replaces it.
- **workflow entry:** Fire one matching event and one event that should not enrol. Inspect both logs.
- **workflow exit:** Cancel or complete the relevant decision and check pending work stops appropriately.
- **sms:** Test a permitted fictional recipient, no phone and do-not-disturb without contacting customers.
- **email:** Inspect sender identity, recipient, merge values, consent and a missing-email path.
- **calendar:** Check host, location, duration, buffers, notice and an unavailable slot.
- **cancellation:** Cancel a test appointment and inspect capacity plus pending reminders.
- **reschedule:** Move a test booking and inspect the old run and new timing.
- **pipeline:** Compare stages and outcomes with the actual decision and verify the responsible owner.
- **payments:** Inspect product, amount and one-time/recurring terms in a safe test context; do not create a real charge.
- **tracking:** Identify the event and denominator used for each conversion; distinguish a page visit from a lead.
- **desktop:** Complete the visitor path with a keyboard at desktop width.
- **tablet:** Complete the same path with a tablet-sized viewport and inspect labels and layout.
- **mobile:** Complete the path at a narrow phone width, including validation and recovery.
- **edge cases:** Test late booking, duplicate entry, missing data and a changed location; record unresolved cases.

## A test record is useful after the build

Keep a concise record of what was tested and what remains unknown. That record supports handoff and makes a later failure reproducible. It is not a fabricated customer outcome. If you cannot establish a required condition, hold launch and name the owner of the unresolved check. The pressure exercise asks you to produce this record from your own run, including a release decision.
