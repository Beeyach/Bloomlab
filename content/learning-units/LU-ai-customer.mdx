# Bound Conversation AI and Voice AI

## Same business boundary, different channels

Conversation AI serves contacts on supported messaging channels. Its Suggestive mode drafts for a human; Auto-Pilot can respond automatically. Voice AI handles voice interactions with configured information and actions. A bot that answers a test chat correctly has not established telephone routing, audio quality or working-hours behavior.

Choose one narrow initial job such as answering approved opening-hours questions and collecting a callback request. Identify the supported channel, owner, source of truth, permitted actions and stop conditions. Verify the intended account's current builder and routing configuration. A phone number assignment or autonomous mode can expose real people to the system; changing either requires approval.

## Handover is a complete path

Escalate when the customer requests a person, the knowledge source is missing or contradictory, an action is outside scope, or the situation is sensitive. A handover label alone is insufficient. Define the destination, opening hours, timeout and what happens when nobody answers. Do not promise that a person is available unless the routing path establishes it.

For this training policy, an unanswered transfer creates a callback task and gives a truthful expectation; it never loops endlessly between bots. Collect only necessary contact details. Explain recording or AI use under the business's applicable policy, and have the owner verify local obligations before deployment.

## Test the consequence, not just the sentence

Rehearse a correct FAQ, an unknown price, a request for a human, an unavailable transfer destination and a caller correcting a detail. Confirm the actual appointment or task state before claiming success. Keep channel-specific permissions and usage costs visible.

The practical is an authored transcript/routing review with no live call, bot or provider. Bloomlab's Call Room and AI settings are separate product features; completing this lesson neither activates HighLevel Voice AI nor changes the parked human voice acceptance.
