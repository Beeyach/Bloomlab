# Separate learning content, entitlement and portal access

## Four connected but different concerns

Courses organise instructional products and lessons. Membership access determines what a particular customer may consume. Communities provide groups, channels and posts with their own membership and moderation decisions. Client Portal brings supported customer resources into a shared access experience. One login does not mean every resource is public.

HighLevel's current portal and membership documentation describes a unified experience, while legacy settings and migration paths may still be visible. Review the actual account, domain and customer access before changing a live portal. A template copy is not a tested migration of credentials, entitlements or learner history.

## Authentication does not grant content

A magic link authenticates its intended recipient; it does not independently grant permission to a course or community. Treat the link as a private, time-limited access mechanism. Never paste one into public demonstration evidence. Test as the intended customer, not only as an administrator who can see everything.

Define the purchase, manual grant, cancellation and refund policies separately. Under the fictional contract below, purchase grants Course A and Group A, while refund requires a reviewed revocation decision. Do not assume that every payment event automatically enforces the desired access policy across every product.

## Test the boundary cases

Check a first-time customer, an expired link, a customer without the offer, a cancelled subscription and a person invited only to one group. Verify what remains accessible after a change, including direct links. An attractive portal page is not proof of isolation.

Keep moderation ownership, content updates and support recovery in the handoff. Courses and communities are ongoing operations, not just launch assets. This practical repairs an entitlement matrix and an expired-link response with synthetic records. It creates no real course, member, portal login or subscription and makes no claim about existing learner progress.
