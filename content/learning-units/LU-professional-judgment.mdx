# Make the smallest defensible commitment

A builder is responsible for deciding whether to build at all. A functioning form with almost no traffic points to a different intervention from a form that loses qualified enquiries. Automation should remove repeatable work with known rules and a recovery owner; an unusual dispute may deserve a person. GHL may support marketing and booking while another system owns clinical or specialist records. A platform's flexibility is not evidence that every kind of data belongs there.

Tags label membership. Typed custom fields hold facts about an entity. Account values hold reusable account-level information. Related records represent things with their own identity and lifecycle. Start with what the fact means and who owns it before choosing a convenient storage control. A many-pets-per-owner problem should not become an unlimited set of pet-name fields. If a native relationship is unverified, name the boundary and investigate it before committing.

Custom code creates maintenance and failure obligations. First test whether supported native controls satisfy the actual requirement. Add complexity only when it solves a demonstrated problem that the simpler design cannot. Two services with different copy may need data-driven wording rather than six nearly identical branches. Trace late bookings, missing phones, duplicate entry and reschedules through the proposed design. Identify who recovers when the automated route cannot run.

Commercial judgment uses the same evidence discipline. Contact a prospect when there is a relevant problem, plausible capacity and an appropriate relationship; decline a poor fit. An audit claim should say exactly what was observed. One delayed reply does not establish a systemic failure rate. Accept a project only when scope, access, capability, time and price allow responsible delivery. Refuse guaranteed outcomes you cannot control. A lower budget may require a smaller release, not omitted safety tests.

A realistic scope names deliverables and exclusions. A realistic price accounts for effort, revisions, QA, risk and support. A realistic complexity budget accounts for maintenance after you leave. When the evidence is incomplete, say what you know, what remains unknown, who can verify it and how the answer changes the decision. “I do not know yet” followed by a precise verification plan is more competent than invented certainty.

Before launch, ask what could break and what was never tested. A desktop happy path leaves mobile, reschedule and failure recovery unresolved. Hold a release when required checks are missing. The practical below presents fourteen separate decisions. Write a recommendation, evidence boundary and next test for each. Several have more than one defensible implementation; the quality rubric rewards reasoning and verified uncertainty rather than a keyword or confident tone.
