# Coach Lead Path

## Demonstration Build — not a client outcome

This demonstration serves a fictional coaching offer with a discovery-call decision, not an instant promise of acceptance. The manifest includes an application survey, fit fields, a pipeline with Review and Invited stages, a discovery calendar and separate invitation and reminder workflows. Record the applicant's stated goal and constraints without treating free text as verified fact.

Under this training policy, staff review every application before an invitation. A simple form submission is not an automatic qualification decision. A staff stage change to Invited releases the calendar link; Declined receives the agreed respectful closure and no booking chase. Booking stops invitation follow-up. Avoid invented revenue, health or transformation claims in copy.

Separate offer-wide copy from client-specific values. A coach changing availability should update the calendar, not hard-code appointment times into each message. The system excludes course delivery, payment collection and membership access; those are distinct scopes with distinct entitlement and refund rules.

The demonstration metric is the count reaching each reviewed stage, with elapsed review time and reasons for non-invitation. Do not declare applications to be qualified opportunities before the human decision. Rehearse an incomplete application, a declined application, a late review and an accepted booking. The most important failure is bypassing review because the automation can technically send a link.

## Reuse with evidence

This is an explicit Bloomwired training blueprint. Repair its supplied manifest and trace the failure case below; the shared runner saves the answers and deterministic evidence. It does not import a HighLevel snapshot or execute outbound work. A local pass verifies only these supplied contracts. A real deployment still needs permission, destination-specific QA and honest proof. Never present this fictional system as a paid engagement, live result or customer testimonial.
