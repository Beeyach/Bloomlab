# Separate agency operations from client accounts

## Start with ownership

An agency's internal operations and a client's business are different security and commercial boundaries. HighLevel's Agency Sub-Account designation identifies an agency-owned workspace; it does not turn the location into every client's account. Keep the customer's data, operational responsibilities and access review explicit.

Draw an account inventory before adding automation: purpose, legal business owner, administrators, supported users, integrations, billing owner and exit plan. Grant access for the job and selected location. An external copywriter needs the agreed content access, not agency-wide administration merely because an administrator invited them. Review access when work ends and document who can revoke it.

## Usage is not unlimited support

Separate platform subscription, communications usage, integration charges and your maintenance fee. State who monitors each meter, who receives warnings and who approves additional spend. Do not quote a platform entitlement from memory; verify the actual plan and permissions in the intended account.

Changes need a decision owner. A contractor can propose a new workflow, but proposal access is not approval to activate messages in every location. Record the destination and dependencies with each release. Keep recovery evidence and give the client an intelligible handoff rather than a list of unexplained internal IDs.

## Design for separation and exit

Avoid making the agency's internal billing workspace a client's operational dependency unless that arrangement is explicitly supported and agreed. Current HighLevel documentation describes transfer restrictions on Agency Sub-Accounts. Confirm eligibility before promising account transfer; never promise that copying a snapshot transfers customer history or every integration.

In the fictional practical, identify the correct workspace and restrict a contractor's access. The answers are an objective boundary plan under supplied constraints, not live permission changes. SaaS Mode, white-label distribution and Marketplace operations have a separate specialist lesson. They are not prerequisites for a small client deployment or the Field Ready certificate.
